About

A little about me

I'm a Materials & Metallurgical Engineering graduate from BUET, currently an Assistant Engineer in the Quality department at Anwar Ispat Ltd., with a strong technical foundation in steel processing, failure analysis, and material characterization. Industry internships and academic research have shaped my interest in production operations, quality control, and raw-material sourcing across the steel and energy sectors. Alongside the technical work, leadership roles have sharpened my organizational, communication, and teamwork abilities — and I'm committed to continuous learning where materials science, engineering judgment, and process optimization converge.

Research & Projects

Effect of Calcium (Ca) Doping on the Dielectric Properties of Barium Titanate (BaTiO3)

S. Ainan, A. N. Ushna, M. M. Rahman · ICME, BUET — via SSRN · 2026

Undergraduate thesis on how calcium (Ca) addition influences the dielectric properties of barium titanate (BaTiO3), a widely used perovskite ceramic. Presented at ICME (International Conference on Mechanical Engineering), BUET. Supervisor: Md. Miftaur Rahman, Assistant Professor, Dept. of MME, BUET.

Read on SSRN Google Scholar ResearchGate

Failure Analysis of Grade B500DWR TMT Steel Rebar

Project · Rebend-test failure investigation

Traced the root cause of rebar failure in the rebend test through visual inspection, chemical composition, microstructure, and mechanical testing — finding that faulty rib design produced brittle zones during quenching.

Accelerated Thinning in a High-Pressure Gas Pipeline

Project · Service-life investigation

Investigated why a high-pressure gas pipeline in Rajshahi saw its service life fall from 10 years to 4 due to thinning effects.

Heat-Treatment Design for 304 Stainless Steel

Case study · Aerospace application

Designed an optimized heat-treatment cycle for 304 austenitic stainless steel to enhance strength and corrosion resistance for aerospace components.

Design of a Cement Production Plant

Project · Plant design

End-to-end plant layout: site selection, plot plan, equipment design, environmental factors, and economic feasibility.

A little about me

I'm Sartaz Ainan, a researcher in Materials and Metallurgical Engineering at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). My work centers on dielectric materials, ceramics, and semiconductors — and I enjoy making science more accessible by sharing it through video and social media.

02 / Research & Projects

Published thesis & engineering projects

★ Featured · Publication SSRN · 2026

Effect of Calcium (Ca) Doping on the Dielectric Properties of Barium Titanate (BaTiO3)

S. Ainan · A. N. Ushna · M. M. Rahman — ICME, BUET

Undergraduate thesis on how calcium addition influences the dielectric properties of barium titanate (BaTiO3), a widely used perovskite ceramic. Presented at the International Conference on Mechanical Engineering (ICME), BUET. Supervisor: Md. Miftaur Rahman, Assistant Professor, Dept. of MME, BUET.

Project · Failure analysis

Failure Analysis of Grade B500DWR TMT Steel Rebar

Traced rebend-test failure via visual inspection, chemical composition, microstructure and mechanical testing — faulty rib design produced brittle zones during quenching.

Project · Service-life study

Accelerated Thinning in a High-Pressure Gas Pipeline

Investigated why a high-pressure gas pipeline in Rajshahi saw its service life fall from 10 years to 4 due to thinning effects.

Case study · Aerospace

Heat-Treatment Design for 304 Stainless Steel

Designed an optimized heat-treatment cycle for 304 austenitic stainless steel to enhance strength and corrosion resistance for aerospace components.
